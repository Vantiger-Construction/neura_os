Neura for Windows · Spoken replies
November 19, 2026

1. Close any open Neura window. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open START_NEURA.exe in the extracted folder. Use this launcher each time.
3. In ordinary chat, type “Hello Neura” and send it. You should hear her reply.
   You can hear a voice sample under Settings > Neura's Voice by pressing
   the play button beside an installed voice.

The launcher saves After reply on your Windows account. It keeps As it arrives
if you already selected it. It keeps your existing audible voice volume and
restores a previously muted Neura volume to 70%. You can mute Neura while you
chat. Opening START_NEURA again turns spoken replies back on.

Your usual Windows speaker or headphones must be selected and audible. If you
hear nothing, check Neura's Voice > Volume and the Windows volume mixer.
Safe Mode pauses speech. The isolated text-only chat stays text-only. Startup
greetings and microphone listening have separate controls in Neura's Voice.

To use the saved settings without re-enabling sound at launch, open
Windows\neuro_companion.exe directly. You can then choose Off under
Settings > Neura's Voice > Automatic reply speech.
