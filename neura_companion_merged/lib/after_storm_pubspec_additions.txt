Add this to your pubspec.yaml if you don't already have it:

dependencies:
  shared_preferences: ^2.2.2

Then run:
  flutter pub get

Folder drop:
  - Copy /lib/after_storm, /lib/os_shell/rooms, /lib/weekly/services,
    /lib/care/services, and /lib/shared/services (only if you don't already
    have SelfHealService) into your main app's lib/ directory.

Routing:
  - Wire AfterStormRoom into your OS Shell's room map / navigator.
  - Add a button/tile somewhere (Dashboard or Care Mode) that opens
    AfterStormScreen directly if you want a non-room entry point.

Bridges:
  - Implement real logic in:
      weekly/services/weekly_snapshot_bridge.dart
      care/services/care_mode_bridge.dart
    so AfterStormService feeds into Weekly Snapshot + Care Mode.
